Class Patterns

A class pattern is a way a course can meet during the week: how many credits it is for and its meetings (for example Monday, Wednesday and Friday for 50 minutes each). The scheduler picks from the enabled patterns that match a course's credits. Patterns have no names, so they are numbered by position. Add, edit or remove an individual pattern's meetings on the Meetings page.
